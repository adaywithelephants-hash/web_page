Experience the Magic

Walk alongside gentle giants in the heart of Northern Thailand

More Than Just a Visit

This isn't a tourist attraction — it's a chance to connect. To slow down. To be present with creatures who carry centuries of wisdom in their eyes. Every moment here is a reminder of what it means to respect, protect, and simply be.

A Day With Elephants

Watch them move, graze, and simply exist as they are. There's a quiet kind of beauty here — unhurried, grounding, and deeply real.

Half Day Morning

THB 1,600 / adult Child (4-9 yrs): 1,000 THB
Pickup 07:00 - 07:30 AM
Return 12:30 - 01:00 PM

Half Day Morning Program

Highlights

Step into their world and learn about who they are, where they came from, and how they're looked after — told through a warm, personal introduction.

🍌 Prepare Elephant Food

Mix and shape nutritious treats using fresh fruits and herbs — a special snack made with care.

🤲 Feeding Time

Offer food by hand and share a gentle, close moment together.

🌿 Walk Through Nature

Stroll slowly alongside the elephants through the forest, letting them graze and move at their own pace.

💦 River Bathing

Join them in the river for a refreshing bath — splash, scrub, and enjoy a joyful, memorable experience.

🍽️ Lunch & Shower

Savor a simple Thai lunch at the sanctuary, with showers available to freshen up after the muddy fun.

Half Day Afternoon

THB 1,600 / adult Child (4-9 yrs): 1,000 THB
Pickup 01:30 - 02:00 PM
Return 06:30 - 07:00 PM

Half Day Afternoon Program

Meet the Elephants & Hear Their Stories

Begin with a warm introduction to the elephants — where they came from, how they've recovered, and how your visit helps care for them moving forward.

🍌 Create Elephant Food by Hand

Shape nutritious vitamin treats from fruits, herbs, and grains. It's hands-on, a little messy, and loved by the elephants.

🤲 Feeding Time

Step in close and offer food by hand, experiencing the gentle touch of their trunks and their calm presence.

🌿 Walk Through Nature

Wander slowly through the forest alongside the elephants, letting them lead the way as they stop, graze, and explore freely.

💦 Bathing in the River

Share a peaceful river bath together — rinse, scrub, and splash in a moment that feels both joyful and memorable.

🍽️ Lunch/Supper & Shower

Enjoy a comforting Thai meal at the sanctuary, with time to freshen up and relax before the journey back to the city.

Full Day

THB 2,500 / adult Child (4-9 yrs): 1,500 THB
Pickup 07:30 - 08:00 AM
Return 04:00 - 04:30 PM

Full Day Program

✨ Highlight

Walk quietly beside these gentle giants, prepare food with your hands, and share time with them in the river.

Elephant Welcome & Stories

Begin the day with a gentle introduction, learning about each elephant's journey, where they came from, how they're recovering, and how your presence helps support their care.

🍌 Preparing Elephant Treats

Help create their favorite snacks from bananas, sugarcane, and vitamin-rich mixes, then offer them by hand — a simple, joyful moment of connection.

🌿 Walking Side by Side

No saddles, no chains, no rush. Just a slow walk through forest trails and hillside paths, following as they roam, pause, and explore at their own pace.

🍽️ Lunch in Nature

Enjoy a peaceful Thai meal surrounded by trees, fresh air, and the soft sounds of the jungle. Vegetarian options are available.

🧴 Herbal Skin Care or Mud Bath

Take part in caring for their skin with a gentle herbal compress or a cooling mud spa. It's calming, playful, and part of their natural routine.

💦 River Bathing Together

Step into a clear, shallow stream and share a lighthearted bath — washing, splashing, and cooling off together in the flow of the forest.

☕ Optional Shower & Quiet Time

After the day's activities, freshen up in the shower area, rest for a while, and enjoy a warm cup of tea before returning to Chiang Mai.

📋 Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change, and we aim to be as flexible as possible.

Free Cancellation

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled visit for a full refund.

Late Cancellation

  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the program start time will be non-refundable.

No-Show

  • Guests who do not arrive on the scheduled date without notice will be charged 100% of the booking fee.

Rescheduling

  • You may change your date once, free of charge, if requested at least 24 hours in advance and subject to availability.

Weather Conditions

  • In case of severe weather or unsafe conditions, the program may be rescheduled. If rescheduling is not possible, a full refund will be provided.

How to Cancel or Change

  • Please contact us via WhatsApp / Line / Email with your booking name and date.
